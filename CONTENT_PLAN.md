## Content record fields (proposed)
- Title
- Slug
- Content type (note, disease guide, healthcare article, news/update, video)
- Category and subcategory
- Short summary
- Body/content
- Language (English, Hindi, bilingual)
- Author/editor
- References (title, publisher/organisation, URL, publication/update date)
- Medical/pharmacy reviewer (where applicable)
- Last reviewed date
- Published/draft status
- Featured image and alt text
- Related content

## Content quality rules
- Separate general education from personal medical advice.
- Cite reliable primary or authoritative sources.
- Show publication and last-reviewed dates.
- Avoid unsupported treatment claims and generic drug-dose advice.
- Review disease and medicine information before publishing.
- Keep English and Hindi versions aligned and reviewed.
